Prices are sampled at each scan (about every 20 seconds), so a fast spike can be missed. A flat alert counts at its price when the window ends, a loss at the price where the stop was seen. Slippage and your own entry delay are not included. For a much bigger sample than live alerts, run node screener-backtest.js.
How it works: every ~20 seconds the scanner compares each token's last 15 minutes of volume with that token's own normal for this hour of the day (14-day median), so busy hours do not look like surges. An ignition needs: high 15m volume, 1h volume confirming, volume that held across the last 3 five-minute candles with many trades (not one big print), enough aggressive buying, price only just turning up, above a rising 4-hour average and still near its spike high without long selling wicks, BTC not falling over the last hour, a tight spread, and no market-wide surge. Tokens whose typical 6-hour range is too small to reach the target are skipped, as are stablecoins, tokenized stocks and gold, and wrapped tokens. These are combined into an ignition score (0-100) and only scores above the threshold alert. Daily setups are shown as a watchlist; their alerts are off unless SCREENER_SETUP_ALERTS=1. Tokens that already ran are capped at score 55 and never alert. Each ignition also shows its narrative's rank from the BTC rotation tab, and the performance panel tracks whether that rank helps. Demo mode data is simulated.
Read this first: rankings come from daily price history, measured week by week against BTC. Narrative lists and Binance listings are today's, so coins that died or were delisted are missing, which makes every narrative look better than it really was. A few years of data hold only a few dozen weeks of each BTC regime, so treat "low" confidence as noise. Not financial advice.

How it works: every day BTC's daily price action is put into one of six regimes (breaking out, calm uptrend, uptrend, sideways, downtrend, falling hard). Each narrative is an equal-weight index of the largest coins carrying one of Binance's sector tags (AI, Meme, RWA, Gaming and so on). For every past day the screener records how each narrative did over the next week compared with BTC, grouped by the regime BTC was in. The ranking blends that history with current price action, and the backtest replays the same ranking week by week with fees, using only weeks that had already finished. Run node rotation-backtest.js for the full report in a terminal.
