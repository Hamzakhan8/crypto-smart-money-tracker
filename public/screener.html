Signal honesty: Alerts fire within a minute or two of volume igniting, never before it.
Many early signals still fail, and no setting makes most of them win. Whale flows and unlock dates are not checked, so check them yourself before acting. Not financial advice.

Waiting for the first scan… the first run loads daily candles and can take a minute.
How it worksevery ~20 seconds the scanner compares each token's rolling 15-minute volume with its own normal for this hour of the day (14-day median), so busy hours do not look like surges. For the hottest tokens it then checks the last two finished 5-minute candles: an ignition needs both of them at 3× normal volume or more, price up over those 10 minutes, mostly aggressive buying, 1h volume confirming, and the quality checks that held up in the replay: daily RSI 65 or lower, the last candle not an exhausted spike that closed at its high, not the top tick of the 2-hour range, above a rising 4-hour average and not stretched, no long selling wicks, BTC not falling over the last hour or 4 hours, a tight spread, and no market-wide surge. Early ignition alerts fire as soon as that passes; confirmed ignition alerts also need an ignition score of 65+ (it rewards volume that keeps running), which the replay found more accurate but usually a few minutes later. Tokens whose typical 6-hour range is too small to reach the target are skipped, as are stablecoins, tokenized stocks and gold, and wrapped tokens. Daily setups are shown as a watchlist; their alerts are off unless SCREENER_SETUP_ALERTS=1. Tokens that already ran are capped at score 55 and never alert. Every alert is tracked as a trade: take profit at the target, stop at the stop, otherwise sell when the window ends, judged on 1-minute candles and counted after fees and slippage (see the performance panel). Demo mode data is simulated.

How it works: every day BTC's daily price action is put into one of six regimes (breaking out, calm uptrend, uptrend, sideways, downtrend, falling hard). Each narrative is an equal-weight index of the largest coins carrying one of Binance's sector tags (AI, Meme, RWA, Gaming and so on). For every past day the screener records how each narrative did over the next week compared with BTC, grouped by the regime BTC was in. The ranking blends that history with current price action, and the backtest replays the same ranking week by week with fees, using only weeks that had already finished. Run node rotation-backtest.js for the full report in a terminal.

Data health

Where the data comes from right now.

How it works: the tracker reads every new Ethereum block from public RPC nodes (no key) and keeps every token or ETH transfer worth at least the minimum, priced with DexScreener. Tokens without real DEX liquidity are ignored, so fake tokens cannot create fake whales. Senders and receivers are matched against the label list; a transfer into an exchange is a deposit, out of one a withdrawal, between two wallets of the same exchange an internal move (hidden by default). Exchange deposits usually arrive at a customer deposit address first and show here when the exchange sweeps them to its hot wallet. Alerts: large transfers, non-stablecoin exchange deposits and withdrawals, stablecoin mints, burns and issuer moves, and anything on your watchlist.
Research screen, not a prediction: +100% is a research target.
Coins are listed only when their data passes the rules, and every listed coin is saved as an unchangeable snapshot whose real returns are measured afterwards. Most early signals fail. The score is not a probability of doubling. Futures data is context only, and token unlocks and catalysts count only when you have verified them (data/early2x-events.json). Nothing is traded. Not financial advice.

Data health

Missing data earns no points and blocks high priority.

How it works: every few minutes the screen reads Binance spot data for USDT pairs with a verified market cap of $10M-$1B (Binance's circulating supply × price, cross-checked with CoinGecko), at least $1M of 24h volume and a spread of 0.30% or less. Stablecoins, wrapped and staked tokens, tokenized stocks and leveraged tokens are left out. Model A (quiet accumulation): 24h volume at least 2× its 7-day baseline, with more trades, held over several hours, while price stays within a band set by the coin's own daily range. Pre-breakout is the same, pressing a tested 7-day high. Model B (early ignition): the last 3+ five-minute candles each at 2×+ normal volume for that hour, accelerating, with aggressive buying and price rising. Evidence is grouped into five independent families (activity, executed buying, structure, order book, strength vs BTC). Volume and trade count, CVD and buy share, and large-trade buying measure overlapping things, so each group counts once. High priority needs 3+ families, a score of 50+ and passing liquidity, data, extension, BTC and supply checks (a market cap that Binance and CoinGecko disagree on, or under 40% of supply circulating, blocks it). Coins already stretched beyond limits that scale with their own volatility go to "Already moved". Those are tracked too, as a control group. All settings are EARLY2X_* in .env; Telegram alerts stay off until EARLY2X_ALERTS=1.
