Prices are sampled at each scan (about every 20 seconds), so a fast spike can be missed. A flat alert counts at its price when the window ends, a loss at the price where the stop was seen. Slippage and your own entry delay are not included. For a much bigger sample than live alerts, run node screener-backtest.js.
How it works (v5): every ~20 seconds the scanner compares each token's rolling 15-minute volume with its own normal for this hour of the day (14-day median), so busy hours do not look like surges. For the hottest tokens it then checks the last two finished 5-minute candles: an ignition needs both of them at 3× normal volume or more, price up over those 10 minutes, mostly aggressive buying, 1h volume confirming, and the quality checks that held up in the replay: daily RSI 65 or lower, the last candle not an exhausted spike that closed at its high, not the top tick of the 2-hour range, above a rising 4-hour average and not stretched, no long selling wicks, BTC not falling over the last hour or 4 hours, a tight spread, and no market-wide surge. Early ignition alerts fire as soon as that passes; confirmed ignition alerts also need an ignition score of 65+ (it rewards volume that keeps running), which the replay found more accurate but usually a few minutes later. Tokens whose typical 6-hour range is too small to reach the target are skipped, as are stablecoins, tokenized stocks and gold, and wrapped tokens. Daily setups are shown as a watchlist; their alerts are off unless SCREENER_SETUP_ALERTS=1. Tokens that already ran are capped at score 55 and never alert. Demo mode data is simulated.

How it works: every day BTC's daily price action is put into one of six regimes (breaking out, calm uptrend, uptrend, sideways, downtrend, falling hard). Each narrative is an equal-weight index of the largest coins carrying one of Binance's sector tags (AI, Meme, RWA, Gaming and so on). For every past day the screener records how each narrative did over the next week compared with BTC, grouped by the regime BTC was in. The ranking blends that history with current price action, and the backtest replays the same ranking week by week with fees, using only weeks that had already finished. Run node rotation-backtest.js for the full report in a terminal.

How it works: the tracker reads every new Ethereum block from public RPC nodes (no key) and keeps every token or ETH transfer worth at least the minimum, priced with DexScreener. Tokens without real DEX liquidity are ignored, so fake tokens cannot create fake whales. Senders and receivers are matched against the label list; a transfer into an exchange is a deposit, out of one a withdrawal, between two wallets of the same exchange an internal move (hidden by default). Exchange deposits usually arrive at a customer deposit address first and show here when the exchange sweeps them to its hot wallet. Alerts: large transfers, non-stablecoin exchange deposits and withdrawals, stablecoin mints, burns and issuer moves, and anything on your watchlist.
