Each analysis is a four-member AI committee: 3 Gemini requests, 4 when the trader says TAKE (the final boss). Results are kept for 3 minutes.
How it decides: code first builds a dossier from live data and computes every number (trend, ATR, support and resistance, reward:risk, order-book depth, futures crowding, exchange flows). Then four AI members work in turn: the data analyst reports data quality, evidence and red flags; the news researcher searches the web for delistings, hacks, unlocks and listings (with sources); the trader decides with both reports, under rules that include how often this kind of signal won in the past; and a TAKE goes to the final boss (a stronger model), who says YES only if every test passes. Before the boss, code enforces: TAKE only for coins that are igniting now, with expected value of +0.5% or more per trade and reward:risk of at least 1.5, never for coins that already ran or with a hard stop (also a confirmed bad news item); plans must fit the levels; confidence is capped for early signals, demo data and weak markets. If the boss cannot answer, there is no TAKE. Long only, no leverage, risk at most 1% of your account per trade. This is an AI opinion on data, not financial advice.
