How it works: every ~20 seconds the scanner compares each token's last 15 minutes of volume with its own normal (20-day average). It alerts only when 15m volume is high, 1h volume confirms, at least ~58% of recent volume was aggressive buying, price has only just turned up, and the token is not already up a lot in 24h, 1h or 15m. A second tier alerts on strong daily setups (volume, near the 20-day high, hot narrative, MC/FDV, futures, liquidity) that have not moved. Tokens that already ran are capped at score 55 and never alert. The "since alert" figures are sampled at each scan, so treat peaks as approximate. Demo mode data is simulated.
